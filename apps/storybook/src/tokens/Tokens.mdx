# Tokens

The tokens of `@bram-van-osta/tokens`, read from its `tokens.json` and drawn with its CSS variables: this page shows what a product gets.

## Colors

Three roles. Delft blue acts, chooses and moves forward. Vermilion calls for attention, rarely: one or two touches per screen, plus the tulip, and never a button. Ink carries the text and the structure. Each color in the light theme and in the dark theme.

In Tailwind, every color utility: `bg-delft`, `text-ink-muted`, `border-line`, `fill-tulip-flower`.

## Type

Newsreader for the name in the logo only, Inter Tight for the headings, Inter for the text, IBM Plex Mono to situate. The reading column is 720 px at most.

In Tailwind, the family and the style: `font-display text-heading-xl`. A style sets the size, the line height, the weight and the letter spacing.

## Spacing

Room to breathe: one thing at a time, sections apart by `space-7` to `space-9`.

In Tailwind, by name, next to the numeric scale: `p-space-5`, `gap-space-3`.

## Sizes

Every target is 44 px high at least, the primary button 56 px.

In Tailwind, with the spacing and size utilities: `min-h-target-min`, `h-button-height`, `max-w-reading-width`.

## Radii

Square corners everywhere. Only the avatars, the numbered dots and the tile of the icon are rounded. No shadow in the interface.

In Tailwind: `rounded-none`, `rounded-round`, `rounded-icon`.
