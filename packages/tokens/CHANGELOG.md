# @bram-van-osta/tokens

## 0.7.0

### Minor Changes

- The toasts stack, three at most: the newest in front, the two before it smaller behind, unfolded into a column on hover or from the keyboard (F6), a fourth sending the oldest away; they come up and fade in, go down and fade out, slide to their place and can be swiped away, with a fade alone under reduced motion; on a computer they take the new `toast-width` size token (420 px).

## 0.6.0

### Minor Changes

- The `agent` and `agent-pale` color tokens, in light and dark: violet for what the agent read, advised or drafted, on the operator's side only, never on the client's side and never a button.

## 0.5.0

### Minor Changes

- The `icon-button-compact` size token, 28 px: the drawn square of a compact icon button in a line of text, whose target stays `target-min`.

## 0.4.0

### Minor Changes

- 604d059: A new size token, `progress-height` (3 px): the height of a progress bar's segments, across the page under its header.

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- A new size token, `account-name-width` (160 px): the maximum width of the account's name in the header's account button.

## 0.2.1

### Patch Changes

- The usage notes of `vermillion` and `border-attention` name their new uses: the icon of an error and the top rule of a form alert.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- `form-width` (420 px), the width of a short form on a computer; the authentication blocks keep their fields and buttons within it, under a heading and a lead that keep the reading column.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- The Bram van Osta tokens: CSS variables in light and dark, a Tailwind 4 theme, the fonts and the JSON.
