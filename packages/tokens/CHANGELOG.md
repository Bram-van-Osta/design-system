# @bram-van-osta/tokens

## 0.4.0

### Minor Changes

- 604d059: A new size token, `progress-height` (3 px): the height of a progress bar's segments, across the page under its header.

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- A new size token, `account-name-width` (160 px): the maximum width of the account's name in the header's account button.

## 0.2.1

### Patch Changes

- The usage notes of `vermillion` and `border-attention` name their new uses: the icon of an error and the top rule of a form alert.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- `form-width` (420 px), the width of a short form on a computer; the authentication blocks keep their fields and buttons within it, under a heading and a lead that keep the reading column.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- The Bram van Osta tokens: CSS variables in light and dark, a Tailwind 4 theme, the fonts and the JSON.
