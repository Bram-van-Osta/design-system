Adds `ThumbnailGrid`, a category's files in 4:3 tiles, four columns on a computer and two on a phone, with `ThumbnailTile` (an image that opens), `ThumbnailCheckboxTile` (an image to tick, framed in Delft blue once ticked), `ThumbnailFileTile` and `ThumbnailFileTileLink` (a file by its format) and `ThumbnailTileAwaited` (a file on its way).
