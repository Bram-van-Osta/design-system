`CopyList`, values to copy one by one such as bank details: each row shows its label, its value in IBM Plex Mono and a 44 px button that copies it, then says "Copied" for a moment; its texts come from the package's messages (`copyList.copy`, `copyList.copied`, `copyList.failed`) unless the app gives its own, and an optional `copyValue` copies another text (an IBAN without its spaces).
